About Us

Gulf Testing Solutions (GTS) is an ISO/IEC 17025-2017 Accredited and ISO 9001:2015 certified testing, Inspection, Quality Control and surveying services provider – as well as distributor of globally leading brands of surveying equipment and products. GTS has three core business verticals to meet a range of client needs.

MULTI-DISCIPLINARY LABORATORY SERVICES

We offer a wide range of material & microbiology testing, inspection services and technical consulting for clients in Construction, Infrastructure, Manufacturing, Mining, and Energy sectors. Our current infrastructure composes of an independent central laboratory, two branches and a network of nearly 20 mobile/site labs covering the length and breadth of Oman.

GTS is affiliated with several international standards-setting bodies and is registered with the national governmental and regulatory body, Directorate General of standards and metrology (DGSM) in Oman. We are also approved by most governmental, semi-governmental, municipal bodies, and several large companies in Oman.

SURVEYING SERVICES

Our highly trained personnel, with considerable field experience, offer a full array of surveying solutions as well as surface, subsurface and bathymetric survey services of varying scale and complexity.

The GTS team is specially equipped and certified to undertake survey projects, like Cadastral Surveys, Topographical Surveys, Geodetic Surveys, Engineering Surveys, 3D Surveys, Monitoring Surveys, Aerial Surveys, and Utility Detection Surveys.

SURVEY EQUIPMENT SALES, RENTAL & CALIBRATION SERVICES

We are an authorised distributor for leading surveying and construction equipment’s from GEOMAX, IDS GEORADAR and INFRA MARKER BY BERNTSEN, MICRODRONES, LOADSCAN and also offer Total Stations, GPS/GNSS Receivers and Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) equipment on rent.

Besides access to the latest technology, robust and proven equipment in Oman, as accredited service partners, for leading survey & construction equipment manufacturers, GTS also offers a range of support – including calibration, servicing and repair to keep instruments at optimum performance.
